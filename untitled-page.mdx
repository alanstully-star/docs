WRC2 is the second-highest tier of international rallying. It runs alongside the FIA World Rally Championship (WRC) at the same events, on the same stages, and is open to privateers and teams. Every car in WRC2 is built to the FIA's **Group Rally2** rules.

## What is a Rally2 car?

Rally2 (known as R5 until 2019) is an FIA rally car specification introduced for the 2012 season. Each car starts life as a road car that has been built in large numbers, then gets a full rally conversion.

| Rule | Rally2 requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Drivetrain | Four-wheel drive |
| Engine | Turbocharged petrol, up to 4 cylinders, 1620cc |
| Turbo restrictor | 32mm |
| Minimum weight | 1230 kg |
| Weight to power | 4.2 kg per hp |
| Wheels (gravel) | 15 inch |
| Wheels (asphalt) | 18 inch |
| Road car production | At least 2500 identical units |

Rally2 cars sit just below the top Rally1 cars. They are slower, but cheaper to buy and run, which is why they are also the most popular car in European, regional and national rally championships.

## The main cars

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Škoda Fabia RS Rally2" icon="car-side">
    The most common car in WRC2. Škoda Motorsport takes a Volkswagen Group 2.0L EA888 engine and reduces it to 1620cc. It was approved in 2022 and is homologated until 2032. Robert Virves led the 2026 WRC2 standings in a Fabia RS Rally2 run by Toksport WRT.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Toyota GR Yaris Rally2" icon="car-side">
    Toyota's Rally2 car, approved in 2024 and based on the GR Yaris road car. It uses a 1618cc version of Toyota's G16E-GTS three-cylinder turbo engine. In 2026 it was a popular choice for privateers, including Diego Domínguez, who won his home Rally del Paraguay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Lancia Ypsilon Rally2 HF Integrale" icon="car-side">
    The newest car on the list, approved in 2026. It marked Lancia's return to the World Rally Championship. Lancia Corse HF drivers Yohan Rossel and Nikolay Gryazin both won WRC2 rounds in 2026, and the team clinched the WRC2 Teams' Championship in Paraguay.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Ford Fiesta Rally2" icon="car-side">
    Built by M-Sport in Great Britain. Its 1.6L engine makes about 290 bhp. Junior WRC champion Mille Johansson drove one in WRC2 in 2026.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## The 2026 WRC2 season

- 13 rounds, from Rallye Monte Carlo in January to Rally Italia Sardegna in October. Rally Saudi Arabia was dropped from the calendar.
- All crews use Hankook tyres.
- Drivers score from their six best results.
- Titles include WRC2 drivers, co-drivers and teams, plus the WRC2 Challenger championship.
- Oliver Solberg and Elliott Edmondson were the 2025 WRC2 champions.

<Note>
  From 2027, the FIA lets Rally2 makers homologate an optional kit with extra aerodynamic parts and a 10 kg lower weight (1220 kg). This lets Rally2 cars compete in the top WRC category against the new-generation cars.
</Note>
