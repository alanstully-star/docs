## Share your feedback

<Steps>
  <Step title="Talk to the school office">
    Share your feedback in person or by contacting the school directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rate each area">
    Score each area above from 1 to 5 and add a short comment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and publish">
    With your permission, the school adds your feedback to this page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
