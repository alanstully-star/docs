Thank you for considering Tully's Tiny Tots for your little one. Our play school is a home away from home, where children learn, play and grow in a warm, caring space. This page explains how to enroll your child.

## How to enroll

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get in touch">
    Contact the school to ask about available places and to arrange a visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Visit the school">
    Tour our air-conditioned classrooms, meet our teachers and see how children learn through play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the application form">
    Fill in the admission form with your child's details and your contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the required documents">
    Hand in the documents listed below along with the completed form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your child's place">
    Once your application is accepted, complete the admission formalities to confirm your child's place.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle in">
    Your child joins the class and begins their journey with us. We help every child settle in gently so they feel safe and welcome.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Documents required

<Note>
  Confirm the exact list of documents with the school office before applying.
</Note>

- Completed admission form
- Copy of your child's birth certificate
- Recent passport-size photographs of your child
- Proof of address
- Your child's immunization record

## What your child will learn

Our curriculum follows the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 and the National Curriculum Framework for the Foundational Stage (NCF-FS), with a focus on play-based, activity-based learning.
