*The Fast and the Furious* opened in US theaters on June 22, 2001. Directed by Rob Cohen, it put Southern California tuner culture on the big screen and made its cars as famous as its cast.

## Hero cars

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="1995 Mitsubishi Eclipse (Brian)" icon="car-side">
    Brian's first car, a bright green Eclipse with a large aftermarket body kit. He bets it in his street race against Dom, overuses the nitrous and loses. Johnny Tran's gang later destroys it with gunfire, which is why Brian owes Dom a car.

    The car was rented from Los Angeles tuner John Lapid. The car used for close-ups was a non-turbo Eclipse RS, not a turbocharged model.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="1994 Toyota Supra MK IV (Brian)" icon="car-side">
    Brian's replacement for the Eclipse. He brings Dom a run-down Supra from a junkyard and promises a "10-second car." The crew rebuilds it into the orange Supra with large side graphics. At the end of the film, Brian hands Dom the keys instead of arresting him.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="1970 Dodge Charger R/T (Dom)" icon="car-side">
    Dom's black muscle car, built with his late father. It appears in the final drag race against Brian's Supra and is wrecked in the crash that follows.

    Several Chargers were built for filming. A 426 Hemi was used for close-ups, while the other cars used more common big-block 440 engines.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="1993 Mazda RX-7 (Dom)" icon="car-side">
    Dom's red RX-7 FD, which he drives to win the opening street race. It wore a Veilside body kit. The car originally had a roll cage, but it was removed because Vin Diesel could not fit inside with it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Supporting cars

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Volkswagen Jetta (Jesse)" icon="car">
    Jesse races his father's white Jetta against Johnny Tran at Race Wars, loses, and flees instead of handing over the pink slip.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Honda S2000 (Johnny Tran)" icon="car">
    The rival gang leader's car, used to beat Jesse at Race Wars.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nissan 240SX (Letty)" icon="car">
    Letty's S14, which she drives to win her race at Race Wars.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nissan Maxima (Vince)" icon="car">
    Vince's Maxima, fitted with a Vortech supercharger and a Stillen body kit.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Nissan Skyline GT-R R33 (Leon)" icon="car">
    A well-known tuner car before it appeared in the film. It survived filming but was later crushed because of import rules.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Honda Civic EJ1 (heist cars)" icon="car">
    Three black Civics used by Dom's crew to hijack trucks on the freeway in the opening scene.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
