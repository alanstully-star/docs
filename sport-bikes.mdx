Sport bikes are motorcycles built for speed, acceleration, braking and cornering. This section covers three sides of that world: the prototypes raced in MotoGP, KTM's Duke naked bikes, and the superbikes and supersport machines sold for the road.

## Types of sport bike

| Type | What it is | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Racing prototype | Purpose-built race bikes that cannot be bought or ridden on the road | Ducati Desmosedici GP, KTM RC16, Yamaha YZF-M1 |
| Superbike | Full-fairing, roughly 1,000 cc road bikes, often the base for production racing | Ducati Panigale V4, BMW S 1000 RR, Honda Fireblade |
| Supersport and middleweight | Lighter, smaller-capacity faired bikes | Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R, Aprilia RS 660, Yamaha R7 |
| Naked or streetfighter | Sport-bike performance without the full fairing and with a more upright riding position | KTM Duke, KTM Super Duke R, Ducati Streetfighter V4 |
| Entry-level sport | Small-capacity bikes for new and younger riders | KTM RC 390, Yamaha R3, Kawasaki Ninja 500 |
