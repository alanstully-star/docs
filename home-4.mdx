## Getting started

    Get in touch and tell us about your business and the IT help you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assessment">
    We review your current systems, devices and network to understand your requirements.
  </Step>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Onboarding">
    Once you approve the proposal, we set up your services and share how to contact support.

## Requesting support

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Explain what is happening, when it started and what you were doing at the time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Include the details">
    Share your name, the affected device or service, any error messages and a screenshot if possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us the impact">
    Let us know whether one person or the whole business is affected, so we can prioritise correctly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow up">
    We keep you updated until the issue is resolved and confirm with you before closing it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Priority levels

| Priority | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | The whole business cannot work. | Internet or server is down for everyone. |
| High | Several users or a key system is affected. | Email is not working for a team. |
| Medium | One user is affected but can still work. | A printer or application is not working. |
| Low | General questions and planned requests. | Setting up a new user or device. |

<Info>
  Response times for each priority level are set out in your service agreement.
</Info>

## Best practices for users

- **Use strong passwords:** use a unique password for each account and turn on multi-factor authentication where available.
- **Watch for phishing:** do not open unexpected attachments or links, and report suspicious emails to support.
- **Install updates:** restart your computer when prompted so security updates can install.
- **Save files to approved locations:** store work files in backed-up or cloud locations, not only on your desktop.
- **Report problems early:** small issues are quicker and cheaper to fix before they grow.
