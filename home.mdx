## Frameworks & Guidelines

In India, early childhood education is guided by national policies and frameworks, including:

- **National Education Policy (NEP) 2020:** treats ages 3 to 8 as the Foundational Stage and highlights play-based, activity-based learning.
- **National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage (NCF-FS) 2022:** sets out learning goals and practices for children aged 3 to 8.
- **National Early Childhood Care and Education (ECCE) Policy 2013:** promotes holistic care and education for young children.
