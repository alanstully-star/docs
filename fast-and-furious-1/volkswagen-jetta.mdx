**Driver:** Jesse

## In the film

The Jetta is a third-generation (MK3) model that belongs to Jesse's father. At Race Wars, Jesse races it against Johnny Tran's Honda S2000 for pink slips and loses. Instead of handing over the car, he drives off with it.
