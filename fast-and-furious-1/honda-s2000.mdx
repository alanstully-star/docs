**Driver:** Johnny Tran

## In the film

Johnny Tran, the leader of the rival gang, drives the S2000 at Race Wars. He beats Jesse's Jetta in a race for pink slips, but Jesse drives off without handing over his car.

<Note>
  Some sources list this car as a 2000 Honda S2000.
</Note>
