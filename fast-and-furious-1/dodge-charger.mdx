**Driver:** Dom Toretto

**Color:** Black

## In the film

Dom's black muscle car appears in the final drag race against Brian's orange Supra and is wrecked in the crash that follows.

## Behind the scenes

Several Chargers were built for filming. A 426 Hemi was used for close-ups, while the other cars used more common big-block 440 engines. The car destroyed in the crash was not a real Hemi.
