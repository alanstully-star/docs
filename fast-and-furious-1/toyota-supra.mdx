**Driver:** Brian O'Conner

**Color:** Orange

## In the film

After Johnny Tran's gang destroys Brian's Eclipse, Brian owes Dom a car. He brings Dom a run-down Supra from a junkyard and promises "a 10-second car."

The crew rebuilds it into the orange Supra with the "Nuclear Gladiator" side graphics designed by Troy Lee. At the end of the film, Brian hands Dom the keys instead of arresting him.

<Note>
  Some sources list the Supra as a 1995 model. This page uses 1994, the year most commonly cited.
</Note>
