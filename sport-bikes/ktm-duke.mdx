The Duke is KTM's line of naked sport bikes, often called streetfighters. They pair sport-bike engines and chassis with an upright riding position and no full fairing. KTM is based in Mattighofen, Austria, and uses the slogan "Ready to Race."

The first model, the 620 Duke, launched in 1994 with a single-cylinder engine. It was KTM's first four-stroke road bike.

## Current Duke range

| Model | Engine | In production | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 125 Duke | Single cylinder | Since 2010 | Learners and young riders on restricted licenses |
| 200 Duke | Single cylinder | Since 2012 | Budget commuting, popular in India and Asia |
| 250 Duke | Single cylinder | Since 2012 | New riders who want more than 125 cc |
| 390 Duke | Single cylinder | Since 2013 | Light, agile all-rounder and KTM's best-known small bike |
| 790 Duke | Parallel twin | 2017 to 2020, back since 2023 | Middleweight riders who want a sharp, playful chassis. Nicknamed "The Scalpel" |
| 990 Duke | Parallel twin | Since 2024 (990 Duke R added for 2025) | The largest parallel-twin Duke |
| 1390 Super Duke R | V-twin | Since 2024 | Experienced riders. The current flagship, also sold as the 1390 Super Duke R EVO |

## Key features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Lightweight chassis" icon="weight-hanging">
    Dukes use a steel trellis frame and aim for a low weight, which makes them quick to turn and easy to handle in traffic.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Rider electronics" icon="microchip">
    Newer and larger models offer cornering ABS, traction control and selectable ride modes. Features differ between models and markets.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Built with Bajaj" icon="industry">
    The smaller single-cylinder Dukes are developed with KTM's partner Bajaj Auto and built in India, which helps keep them affordable.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Duke vs RC

KTM also sells the RC range, which shares engines with the smaller Dukes but adds a full fairing and a more tucked-in riding position.

|  | Duke | RC |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Style | Naked streetfighter | Fully faired sport bike |
| Riding position | Upright | Sporty and leaned forward |
| Best for | City riding and everyday use | Track days and fast roads |
