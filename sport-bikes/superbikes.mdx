Superbikes are fully faired, roughly 1,000 cc road bikes. They are the closest thing to a race bike you can buy and are the base for the World Superbike Championship (WorldSBK).

## Middleweight sport bikes

Middleweights are lighter and less powerful than superbikes, which makes them easier to use on the road.

| Bike | Engine |
| --- | --- |
| Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R | Inline four |
| Aprilia RS 660 | Parallel twin |
| Yamaha R7 | Parallel twin |
| Triumph Daytona 660 | Inline triple |

## Entry-level sport bikes

These smaller bikes suit new riders and are often the first step toward bigger machines.

| Bike | Engine |
| --- | --- |
| KTM RC 390 | Single cylinder |
| Yamaha R3 | Parallel twin |
| Kawasaki Ninja 500 | Parallel twin |
| Honda CBR500R | Parallel twin |

<Tip>
  If you are new to riding, start with an entry-level or middleweight bike and take a certified riding course before moving up to a superbike.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Model names, engine sizes and availability change by year and by country. Check the manufacturer's site for the current lineup in your market.
</Note>
