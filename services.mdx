Tully's Technologies offers a full range of software services, from building new applications to training your team and keeping your systems running smoothly.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Software development" icon="code">
    We design and build software tailored to your business needs, from internal tools to customer-facing applications.

    - **Custom applications:** web, desktop and mobile applications built around your workflows.
    - **System integration:** connect new software with your existing tools and data.
    - **Modernization:** upgrade or rebuild older systems to improve performance and security.
    - **Ongoing improvements:** add features and refine your software as your business grows.

    **How it works:** we gather your requirements, agree a plan and timeline, build in regular stages with your feedback, and hand over the finished software with documentation.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Software training" icon="chalkboard-user">
    We help your team get the most out of the software they use every day.

    - **User training:** hands-on sessions that teach staff how to use applications confidently.
    - **Administrator training:** guidance for the people who manage settings, users and permissions.
    - **Onboarding:** introductory training for new starters and new systems.
    - **Training materials:** easy-to-follow guides and reference documents your team can keep.

    **How it works:** we assess your team's current skills, plan sessions around their roles, and deliver training on-site or online.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Software testing" icon="vial-circle-check">
    We check that your software works correctly, performs well and is secure before it reaches your users.

    - **Functional testing:** confirm every feature works as expected.
    - **Regression testing:** make sure new changes do not break existing features.
    - **Performance testing:** check speed and stability under real-world workloads.
    - **User acceptance testing:** help your team confirm the software meets their needs before launch.

    **How it works:** we create a test plan, run the tests, report any issues with clear steps to reproduce them, and retest once fixes are in place.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Support" icon="headset">
    We keep your software running smoothly after launch, so your team can focus on their work.

    - **Help desk:** a single point of contact for questions and problems.
    - **Bug fixes:** investigate and resolve issues quickly.
    - **Updates and maintenance:** apply updates and security patches to keep software current.
    - **Monitoring:** spot and address problems before they affect your users.

    **How it works:** contact support with a description of the issue, we prioritise it based on its impact, and we keep you updated until it is resolved.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
