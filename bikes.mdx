## Sport bikes overview

Sport bikes are motorcycles built for speed, acceleration, braking and cornering. They fall into five main types:

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Racing prototype | Ducati Desmosedici GP, KTM RC16, Yamaha YZR-M1 |
| Superbike | Ducati Panigale V4, BMW S 1000 RR, Honda Fireblade |
| Supersport and middleweight | Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R, Aprilia RS 660, Yamaha R7 |
| Naked or streetfighter | KTM Duke, KTM Super Duke R, Ducati Streetfighter V4 |
| Entry-level sport | KTM RC 390, Yamaha R3, Kawasaki Ninja 500 |

## MotoGP

MotoGP is the top class of Grand Prix motorcycle racing, a world championship that began in 1949. Its bikes are prototypes that cannot be bought by the public.

- **Manufacturers:** Ducati (Desmosedici GP), Aprilia (RS-GP), KTM (RC16), Honda (RC213V) and Yamaha (YZR-M1).
- **2026 grid:** 11 teams and 22 riders, in the last season of 1,000 cc engines. From 2027, engines drop to 850 cc.
- **Top speed record:** 368.6 km/h, set by Jorge Martín (Aprilia) at Mugello in 2026.
- **Race weekend:** practice on Friday, qualifying and the Sprint on Saturday, and the Grand Prix on Sunday.
- **Recent champion:** Marc Márquez (Ducati) won the 2025 title.

The full page also covers older MotoGP bikes such as the Honda RC211V and Suzuki GSV-R, recent champions and legends of the sport.

## KTM Duke

The Duke is KTM's line of naked sport bikes, with sport-bike engines, an upright riding position and no full fairing. The first model, the 620 Duke, launched in 1994.

| Model | Engine | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 125, 200 and 250 Duke | Single cylinder | Learners and new riders |
| 390 Duke | Single cylinder | A light, agile all-rounder |
| 790 Duke | Parallel twin | Middleweight riders ("The Scalpel") |
| 990 Duke | Parallel twin | The largest parallel-twin Duke |
| 1390 Super Duke R | V-twin | Experienced riders. The current flagship |

The full page also covers past Duke models, key features and how the Duke compares with KTM's faired RC range.

## Other sport bikes

Superbikes are fully faired, roughly 1,000 cc road bikes and the base for the World Superbike Championship (WorldSBK).

- **Superbikes:** Ducati Panigale V4, Yamaha YZF-R1, Honda CBR1000RR-R Fireblade, Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R, BMW S 1000 RR and Aprilia RSV4.
- **Middleweights:** Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R, Aprilia RS 660, Yamaha R7 and Triumph Daytona 660.
- **Entry-level:** KTM RC 390, Yamaha R3, Kawasaki Ninja 500 and Honda CBR500R.
